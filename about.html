Who is Matthew Hartfield?

Hi, I'm Matt Hartfield. I graduated KVCC with an Associate's in Software Development, and I love both programming and writing.

I was born and raised in Michigan, and I still currently live in the southwest area. I'm the youngest of six brothers, so I've grown accustomed to a large immediate family - especially with over a dozen nieces and nephews now.

In my personal life, I love games that encourage me to strategize or immerse me in a deep story with complex characters. Given my family background, it is no surprise that I also enjoy group activities that allow me to bond with family and have fun. While these group activities are typically recreational in nature, I have done more productive activities in a group setting as well, such as working with one of my brothers on a website or helping my nephews with schoolwork. I also enjoy engaging in various creative projects, such as writing stories, making digital art, and developing video games.

As for this site, you will find all the projects that I have worked on over the past several years. While you will find mostly various code-based projects, I wanted to also include my published written works as creative writing is a passion of mine.
